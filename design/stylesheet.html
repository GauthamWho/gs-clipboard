Gautham's Clipboard

Style sheet and content sheet. Design system "Quiet instrument", clipboard edition, shared with G.ai Token Tray. Code tokens live in Sources/GsClipboard/DesignSystem.swift (DS); wording lives in design/CONTENT.md.

Principles

Light, not loudRegular and medium weights only. Hierarchy comes from size and grey tone, not bold or boxes.
Glass, not blocksNative Liquid Glass on macOS 26, system material on 14–15. The desktop shows through softly. No solid slabs.
Alive under the pointerA soft light follows the cursor, the selection slides on a spring, rows lift a point. All off under Reduce Motion.
Pictures you can readImages get a 120 × 76 thumbnail in the list and a large preview on hover. You know what it is without opening it.
Next to you, never on you⌥V opens just under your text cursor (above it when there is no room), so the line you type in stays visible. The menu bar icon drops it under the icon.
One tintCandy pink (#FF70B3 dark, #CC1A73 light, both ≥4.5:1) marks selection, chips and the primary button. Blue and white belong to the logo only.
8-point rhythmGaps 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20. Nothing crowded, nothing overlapping, no dead space.
Quiet but readablePin and delete appear on hover only. Hints and badges use the meta grey (white 56% / #6E6E73, ≥4.5:1), never tertiary grey.
